--- PAGE 2 ---
Slide 1
Purpose of this plan
Stock Connect uses Channel Engine (CE) as the hub to marketplaces: catalog, stock, price, orders, shipments, returns, 
channels, and invoices flow through the CE Merchant API and our Redis/BullMQ queue layer. This plan defines an in- 
house Marketplace Hub that replaces CE for those flows only — ERP connectors, UI, and seller workflows in Stock 
Connect stay unchanged; we swap the integration target.
Management decision frame
We are not cloning all of ChannelEngine (1,300+ plugins, repricing, settlements UI). We rebuild what our backend 
already calls — eight business domains and thirteen queued operation types — plus marketplace adapters for channels 
our sellers use (Noon, Namshi, Amazon, and others live in production).

Offers Stock & price Sync after ERP or manual updates

Based on Stock Connect codebase audit — Channel Engine integration inventory.

--- PAGE 4 ---
Slide 3
Explicitly out of scope
•  ChannelEngine UI, branding, or full global connector catalogue.
•  CE add-ons never called from Stock Connect (repricing suites, settlement dashboards, affiliate networks).
•  Rewriting ERP modules (Sentos, Shopify, UniCommerce, Kip, etc.) — only the downstream hub changes.
Additional marketplaces or features can follow after parity for the current seller base.

--- PAGE 5 ---
Slide 4
Target architecture (conceptual)
A Marketplace Hub (internal product) exposes operations matching what Stock Connect already performs against CE. 
Stock Connect enqueues work as today; the hub runs marketplace adapters (Noon Partner API, Amazon SP-API, etc.).
•  Hub: sellers, channel registry, jobs, audit, rate limits.
•  Adapters: plug-in per marketplace family.
•  Events: marketplace webhooks normalized to what Stock Connect order webhooks expect.
•  Migration: shadow compare !’ pilot sellers !’ full rollout; CE fallback until 30-day stable KPIs.

--- PAGE 8 ---
Slide 7
M0 — Program charter (2 weeks)
•  Align stakeholders: product, engineering, ops, finance (CE cost baseline).
•  Deliverables: signed scope, RACI, KPIs (publish %, order lag, queue SLA).
•  Prioritized marketplace list from production CE configuration.
•  Risk register: API limits, marketplace agreements, compliance.
Exit: management approval to fund M1–M8 with team and budget.

--- PAGE 9 ---
Slide 8
M1 — Hub foundation (6 weeks)
Platform skeleton for all later modules.
•  Seller & channel models aligned with Stock Connect.
•  Job orchestration: rate limits, retries, dead-letter, ops list/stats (parity with today).
•  Credential vault, API security, audit logs.
•  Dev/staging + synthetic test channels.
Exit: test job runs end-to-end; wave-1 channels registered.

--- PAGE 10 ---
Slide 9
M2 — Catalog module (8 weeks)
•  Product push by channel (batched).
•  Freeze / unfreeze, bulk delete, extra-data bulk update.
•  Read-back by SKU for reconciliation.
•  Wave-1 adapters: Noon, Namshi, Amazon publish/update.
Exit: pilot seller "e99% SKU parity vs CE in staging; ops runbooks.

--- PAGE 11 ---
Slide 10
M3 — Offers: stock & price (5 weeks)
•  Stock sync (offer stock) — same triggers as ERP cron / manual API today.
•  Price sync (offer price) including CSV import batches.
•  Marketplace quota-aware batching and rate limits.
Exit: 24h soak — ERP !’ Stock Connect !’ Hub !’ marketplace matches CE tolerance.

--- PAGE 12 ---
Slide 11
M4 — Orders (6 weeks)
•  Paginated order download (replaces CE GET orders).
•  Acknowledge orders (idempotent if already ack’d).
•  Merchant cancellation to marketplace APIs.
•  Inbound webhooks !’ Stock Connect order handler contract.
Exit: shadow mode order match for pilot seller; webhook latency within SLA.

--- PAGE 13 ---
Slide 12
M5 — Shipments (5 weeks)
•  Create shipment in hub / marketplaces.
•  Delivery state updates.
•  Import merchant shipments (replace CE merchant fetch).
•  Carriers (AyMakan, Omniful) unchanged; hub updates marketplace-facing status.
Exit: sandbox order !’ shipment !’ delivery visible on marketplace.

--- PAGE 14 ---
Slide 13
M6 — Returns (4 weeks)
•  Pull returns from marketplaces.
•  Merchant return create and acknowledge.
•  Accept / reject lines — same behaviour as CE path today.
Exit: pilot channel returns flow; ops/finance sign-off on samples.

--- PAGE 15 ---
Slide 14
M7 — Invoices, channels, operations (3 weeks)
•  Channel list API (replace CE GET channels).
•  Order invoice retrieval for invoice upload.
•  Ops API parity with /api/channel-engine-queue for support.
Exit: support diagnoses hub jobs without CE; staging onboarding works.

Slide 20
Summary for leadership
Recommendation
In 10–12 months with a 4–5 person squad, replace Channel Engine for Stock Connect by building a Marketplace Hub 
covering only the eight domains we use today — direct Noon/Namshi/Amazon (and other live) adapters, not a full 
ChannelEngine clone.
Eight milestones, ~36 person-months, shadow mode and seller-by-seller cutover to limit revenue risk. Stock Connect 
ERP and frontend remain; we gain control, cost transparency, and faster fixes for our sellers.
