1. Executive Summary
Nexora is a multi-tenant modular monolith with a mature commerce core (products, offers, inventory, pricing,
orders, shipments, cancellations, returns), four marketplace adapters (Amazon, Noon, Namshi, Shopify), event-driven
catalog sync, marketplace webhook ingress, inbound/outbound order lifecycle, and a ChannelEngine
Merchant-compatible /api/v2  surface for orders, shipments, cancellations, and returns.
Conclusion: Nexora can extend to replace StockConnect’s ChannelEngine hub without removing or rewriting
existing functionality, but cannot today serve as a drop-in CHANNEL_ENGINE_BASE_URL  swap. The largest gaps are CE
Merchant catalog/offer APIs, Wave-1 marketplace order creation (Amazon/Noon/Namshi), invoices, GET
/channels, CE-style queue administration, Namshi outbound fulfillment, product freeze/bulk-delete/extra-
data, outbound returns, and Trendyol.
Preservation rule: All work in this roadmap is additive and backward-compatible. Existing /api/v1 , marketplace
adapters, workers, and /api/v2  endpoints must remain functional; each milestone includes regression gates ( npm run
verify , integration suite, affected compatibility tests).

13. Data Consistency and Integration Plan
